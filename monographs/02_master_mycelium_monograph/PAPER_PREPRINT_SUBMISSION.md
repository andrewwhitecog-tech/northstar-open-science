## 1. Structured Abstract

Synthesizing 45 mycological chapters and commercial agricultural research from Yamhill County, Oregon (Oregon Tilth Certified Organic OT-011161; ORS 616.706-.726), this monograph examines four interconnected domains of fungal biology and gastronomy: (1) the four-enzyme genetically encodable caffeic acid bioluminescence cycle (HispS, H3H, Luz, CPH; lambda_max ~ 520-530 nm); (2) four elevation-stratified fruiting corridors across the Pacific Northwest Cascadia bioregion; (3) forensic lookalike toxicology incorporating the November 2025 - March 2026 Northern California amatoxin outbreak (CDC MMWR Vol. 75); and (4) the two-stage dry-evaporation and Maillard searing protocol for cross-linked chitin/beta-glucan cell walls at 425°F (218°C), modeling 94.8% retention of the sulfur-histidine antioxidant L-ergothioneine.

**Keywords**: `mycology`, `Cascadia fungi`, `fungal bioluminescence`, `3-hydroxyhispidin`, `Amanita phalloides`, `chitin thermodynamics`, `ergothioneine`, `mycorrhizal glomalin`
