# Summary

This compendium packages the theoretical engineering derivations, ISO-10303-21 STEP solid-model geometries, KiCad 8.0 schematic netlists, and bills of materials across the NorthStar Invention Forge series (MK1 through MK55), accompanied by our automated structural verifier (engineering_integrity.py). In strict compliance with NorthStar's engineering integrity standard, all designs are published with explicit epistemic provenance distinguishing verified physical governing equations and structural file completeness from physical prototype fabrication certification.

# Statement of Need

Reproducible scientific monographs require machine-verifiable numerical bounds, explicit epistemic provenance (`[VERIFIED]`, `[INFERRED]`, `[PROPOSED]`), and deterministic test suites (`pytest`) that prevent silent data drift or unverified extrapolations. This package provides both the complete scholarly synthesis and the companion verification artifacts for `06_invention_forge_open_hardware_compendium`.
