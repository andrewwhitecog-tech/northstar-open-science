# Summary

*The Definitive Book of Hyperbaric Chamber Therapy* is an open-science clinical, biophysical, and engineering monograph and software verification suite covering the full spectrum of Undersea and Hyperbaric Medicine. It unifies: (1) an 80-page typeset 24-chapter textbook (`The_Definitive_Book_of_Hyperbaric_Chamber_Therapy_Full_Book.pdf`), (2) a standards-compliant EPUB 3.0 e-book (`The_Definitive_Book_of_Hyperbaric_Chamber_Therapy.epub`), (3) a 6-page laminated chamber console wall-charts and dive-profile atlas (`Hyperbaric_Clinical_Operations_Wall_Charts_and_Pocket_Cards.pdf`), (4) an 18-page, 50-question ABMS Undersea & Hyperbaric Medicine subspecialty board-review workbook (`ABMS_Hyperbaric_Medicine_50_Question_Board_Review_Workbook.pdf`), and (5) an interactive HTML5 reader and biophysical calculator (`THE_DEFINITIVE_BOOK_OF_HYPERBARIC_THERAPY_INTERACTIVE_EDITION.html`).

# Statement of Need

Hyperbaric medicine suffers from a sharp divide between hospital-based multiplace/monoplace clinical practice (governed by the UHMS 15th Edition Indications Manual, ASME PVHO-1, NFPA 99 Chapter 14, and CMS Medicare NCD 20.29) and commercial wellness claims surrounding mild portable fabric chambers (1.3 ATA). This suite provides a strictly stratified, equation-verified reference separating `VERIFIED` FDA/UHMS indications, `VERIFIED OFF-LABEL RCT` protocols [@epmc_32630465; @epmc_35821512; @epmc_35192645; @epmc_20427683], `INFERRED / PROPOSED` translational mechanisms, and `REFUTED` indications.
